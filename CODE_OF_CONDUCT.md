# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want everyone who interacts with this project — reporting issues,
proposing changes, reviewing code, writing docs, or chatting in the
repository's discussion spaces — to have a good, productive experience.
This code of conduct describes the behavior we expect from maintainers
and contributors alike, and how we respond when that expectation is not
met.

## Expected behavior

- **Be respectful and constructive.** Disagree with ideas, not with
  people. Critique code, not the person who wrote it.
- **Assume good faith.** Bugs, naive questions and repeated reports
  happen; treat them as opportunities to improve the project.
- **Keep it professional.** Keep discussions on-topic, and take
  off-topic or personal conversations elsewhere.
- **Be patient and generous.** Answer questions you can answer, share
  context, and document the things you learn along the way.
- **Respect the scope of the project.** Feature requests are welcome;
  accept that the maintainers may decline them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, insults or derogatory comments about
  anyone or any group of people.
- Discrimination based on age, body size, caste, disability, ethnicity,
  gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality,
  personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation,
  socio-economic status, or any other characteristic protected where you
  live.
- Publishing (or threatening to publish) other people's personal
  information, such as physical or electronic addresses.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider
  inappropriate in a professional setting.
- Trolling, brigading, dogpiling or coordinated bad-faith
  participation.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of
conduct and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response
to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or
harmful.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces — the repository, its
issue tracker and pull requests, discussions, and any event or channel
officially affiliated with the project — and also applies when an
individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior may
be reported to the project maintainer at
**bilalahmed24.dev@gmail.com**. Please include links or transcripts
where possible.

All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All
reports are kept confidential to those who need to see them in order to
resolve the matter. Retaliation against anyone who files a report in
good faith is itself a violation of this code of conduct.

## Consequences

Behavior that violates this code of conduct may result in any action the
maintainers deem appropriate, up to and including a temporary or
permanent ban from the project's spaces, and (for contributors) the
rejection or reversion of contributions.

## Attribution

This policy draws on the norms shared by the wider open-source
community. It is maintained as part of this repository and may be revised
by the maintainers at any time; the version in the repository's default
branch is the one in effect.
